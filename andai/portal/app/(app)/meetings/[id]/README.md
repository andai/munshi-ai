# /meetings/[id]

One meeting (`components/meetings/meeting-view.tsx`): status and when it changed, the failure
reason, how to admit the bot while it waits, the recording notice, a Stop button while the bot is
in, and the transcript as it arrives. Once it has ended: the stored transcript (original script or
Roman Hinglish), attendees and minutes, with Share and Delete for those andai-core allows. A
meeting the account may not read (or a malformed id) shows `not-found.tsx`, "Meeting not found";
so does a meeting just deleted from this page.
